Oopbuy spreadsheet guide

Turn a saved link
into a useful comparison.

An Oopbuy product spreadsheet becomes more useful when every entry follows the same format. Keep observations separate from seller claims, and leave unknown information marked as unknown.

For Oopbuy spreadsheet finds, prioritize details you can check: the source page, selected variant, measurements, price currency and the date you reviewed the listing.

Save the original source

Record the product URL, seller name if shown, category and exact variant. A screenshot can provide context, but retain the source link so you can check for changes.

Compare measurements

Use a well-fitting item you already own as a reference. For clothing, compare garment measurements; for shoes, check the seller’s sizing information and any available length measurements.

Inspect the available evidence

Separate listing images, customer photos and QC photos in your notes. Look for visible differences in color, construction, dimensions and condition.

Build a realistic estimate

Add confirmed charges and label estimates clearly. Recheck availability, service terms, shipping restrictions and return conditions with the relevant provider before paying.

A practical product research sheet structure
Field group What to record Why it helps
Product identity Original URL, title, seller, category and selected variant Keeps similar-looking products and variants distinct.
Size and material Size label, available measurements and stated material Makes fit and specification differences visible.
Photo evidence Listing images, QC references and visible issues Separates what you observed from what was advertised.
Item cost Listed price, currency, quantity and review date Preserves the context of a price comparison.
Additional costs Domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and payment costs Helps prevent an incomplete budget.
Decision notes Questions, return terms, availability and shortlist status Shows what still needs checking before an order.

Budget formula: estimated total = item cost + domestic delivery + service fees + international shipping + payment or currency conversion costs + applicable taxes or duties. Avoid counting charges twice if a provider already includes them.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a spreadsheet research guide and category links to CAFANS. You can use its suggested fields to create your own comparison sheet.

How should I compare prices in a spreadsheet?

Record the item price separately from domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, payment conversion costs and any applicable taxes or duties. Treat the total as an estimate until the relevant charges are confirmed.

What should I check in QC photos?

Compare the pictured item with the listing, requested size, color, visible measurements and obvious condition issues. QC photos can help you inspect visible details but do not independently prove authenticity or long-term durability.
